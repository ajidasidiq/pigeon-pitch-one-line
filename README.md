Entry for the make.fun bounty "Pigeon Pitch, One Line": the one-line
elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup, 200
characters or fewer.

## The entry

```
I'm raising my seed round — literally. I deliver breadcrumbs in under a coo, my fleet works for crumbs, never strikes, and already owns every rooftop in the city.
```

## Run

```
python3 main.py
```

Writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (162 characters, one line).

## Why this line

It is shaped like a real founder pitch (the raise, the product, the
moat), and every beat is a pigeon joke that only a pigeon could tell:
the "seed round" is literal, delivery runs "in under a coo", the fleet
works for crumbs and never strikes, and the unfair advantage is that
the pigeon already owns every rooftop in the city. First person, city
life throughout, and no word wasted.
